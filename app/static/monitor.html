How to read this – and its limits

  • What it measures: how far the new clip is from this machine's own enrolment clips, compared with how far the enrolment clips are from each other (z-score). It does not say which fault it is.
  • Evidence (v2 study): settings chosen on 6 development machines, tested once on id_06: 81 % of faults caught with about 0.9 false alarms per machine per day (2-of-3 rule). Development fans id_00 and id_04 were much weaker (1 % and 9 %).
  • Assumptions: enrolment clips are truly healthy and cover normal operating conditions; the 2-of-3 numbers assume clips are independent, so real false alarms may be higher.
  • Re-enrol after maintenance, a new microphone position or a change of load. Changes in the recording set-up also look "unusual".
  • A screening aid for the maintenance engineer. It never replaces a scheduled inspection, and the test machine id_06 had been used before, so this is not yet proof on a new site.
